# Marbella visual direction

## Positioning

- Premium and trustworthy, not flashy luxury
- Dark blue and muted gold as the main brand system
- Dutch cue through clarity, restraint and copy, not through obvious flag graphics

## Recommended image mix

1. Old Town / Plaza de los Naranjos
   - Use for trust, heritage and local credibility
   - White facades, arches, stone, warm daylight

2. Puerto Banus / marina details
   - Use for exclusivity and Marbella recognition
   - Prefer elegant frames over nightlife-heavy shots

3. Villa lifestyle near the sea
   - Use for the property-management angle
   - Terraces, sea views, calm luxury, no hard-sell real estate look

## Selection notes

- For the hero, start with a calm Marbella scene, not a crowded beach.
- For the second fold, combine Old Town and villa imagery to balance trust and aspiration.
- When the team photo is available, place it in the current reserved block and use natural light with neutral styling.
